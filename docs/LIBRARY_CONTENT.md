## Suggested tiers

- **Small (about 2 GB):** Where There Is No Doctor, the canning guide, FM 21-76, Wikipedia
  Medicine, zimgit water, medicine and post-disaster, Vikidia, the McGuffey readers.
- **Medium (about 5 GB):** Small, plus the other Hesperian books, Appropedia, Energypedia, the
  Stack Exchange sets, PhET and the LibreTexts K-12 set.
- **Large (about 10 GB):** Medium, plus the OpenStax nursing and school textbooks and the remaining
  LibreTexts sets.
